Thesis & Research Direction

The central thread of my work is making neural-network computation more hardware-efficient without losing practical accuracy.

Undergraduate Thesis · Ongoing Multiplierless CNN Acceleration

HeSA-TAPoT: A Boundary-Aware Heterogeneous Systolic Array Architecture for Multiplierless CNN Acceleration Using Two-Term Additive Power-of-Two Quantization

Heterogeneous Systolic Design

Exploring a boundary-aware architecture that uses different processing behavior where dataflow and accumulation requirements change across the array.

Two-Term Additive PoT

Approximating quantized weights using two signed power-of-two terms so multiplication can be replaced by two shifts and an addition.

RTL-Oriented Evaluation

Developing and verifying Verilog RTL while studying trade-offs among parallelism, numerical precision, resource cost, and accelerator throughput.
